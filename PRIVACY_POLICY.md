# SprachCoach Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 2026**

> This document mirrors the privacy policy shown inside the app
> (Settings → Privacy & data → View privacy policy). Host it at a public
> URL (e.g. GitHub Pages) and enter that URL in App Store Connect →
> App Privacy → Privacy Policy URL.

## Overview

SprachCoach is a German-practice app that lets you hold spoken
conversations with an AI tandem partner. This policy explains what data
the app handles, how it is collected, what it is used for, and with whom
it is shared.

## Data we collect ourselves

None. SprachCoach has no server, no user accounts, and no analytics or
tracking of any kind. The developer never receives, stores, or sells any
of your data.

## Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and never reaches the developer:

- your Gemini API key (stored encrypted in the iOS keychain),
- your saved conversation transcripts and their feedback reports,
- your language and coaching-style settings,
- the record of your data-sharing consent.

You can delete each of these inside the app at any time; deleting the app
removes them all.

## Data shared with the Google Gemini API

To power its core features, the app sends data directly from your device
to Google's Gemini API, a third-party AI service operated by Google LLC.
This happens **only after you have given explicit consent in the app**,
and only in these situations:

- **During a live practice call:** your microphone audio (your voice) and
  the live transcript of the conversation.
- **When you request feedback on a saved conversation:** that
  conversation's transcript.
- **If you have written custom coaching instructions:** that text, as
  part of the conversation setup.

**How it is collected:** the microphone is only active during a call you
started, and transcripts are the text of that conversation. The data is
transmitted over an encrypted connection (TLS) using the API key you
provided, under your own Google account. Google processes this data as
described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and the [Gemini API Additional Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

## What the data is used for

Exclusively to provide the app's functionality: running the live voice
conversation, showing the transcript, and generating grammar and
vocabulary feedback you explicitly request. The app uses the data for
nothing else — no advertising, no profiling, no training of the
developer's own models (the developer has none).

## Your consent and your control

The app cannot be used until you have read the data-sharing notice and
agreed to it. You can withdraw this consent at any time in
Settings → Privacy & data; the app then stops working until you agree
again, and nothing further is sent. You can also delete your API key,
individual saved conversations, and your custom instructions at any time.

## Data retention

The developer retains nothing, because the developer receives nothing.
On-device data is kept until you delete it. For how long Google retains
data processed by the Gemini API, please refer to Google's terms linked
above.

## Children

SprachCoach is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not
knowingly process children's data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is shared or with whom,
the app will show the updated notice and ask for your consent again
before sending anything.
